# Google Business Profile

**Start this first.** Verification is by posted card and takes up to two weeks,
which makes it the longest lead time in this folder. For a plant hire company in
Middelburg it will also generate more enquiries than everything else here
combined.

---

## Why it matters more than the website

Search "plant hire Middelburg" on a phone. Above the ordinary results sits a map
with three businesses in it. That is the local pack, and it is fed by Google
Business Profile, not by the website.

A business with no profile cannot appear there at all, no matter how well the
website is built. A business with a complete profile, photographs and reviews
can outrank a far larger competitor locally.

It also decides what shows when someone searches the company by name: the panel
on the right with the phone number, the hours, the directions button and the
"open now" indicator.

## Claiming it

The Facebook page suggests a listing may already exist under a different name.

1. <https://www.google.com/business>
2. Search `Leikah` and `Leikah Plant Hire`, then the address `7 July Street
   Middelburg`.
3. If a listing exists → **Claim this business**.
4. If nothing exists → create one.

**Decide which Google account owns it before you start.** Ownership is painful
to move later. It should be an account Leikah controls — ideally the
`admin@leikahgroup.co.za` mailbox once it exists — not a personal account.

## Verification

Google will offer one or more of: postcard, phone, email, video.

**Postcard** is the usual option for a business with a physical address. A card
with a five-digit code is posted to the yard, arriving in five to fourteen days.
Someone has to be there to receive it, and the code expires after thirty days.

**Video verification** is increasingly offered and is much faster. You record a
continuous walkthrough showing the signage, the yard, the equipment and some
proof of management such as a till or a filing cabinet. If it is offered, take
it.

Nothing about the listing shows publicly until verification completes.

## What to fill in

### Name

Exactly as it appears on the signage and legal documents, with nothing added.
"Leikah Plant Hire" and not "Leikah Plant Hire | Earthmoving Middelburg".
Keyword stuffing the name is against the guidelines and gets listings suspended.

**This is blocked.** Three names are in circulation: "Leikah Plant Hire" on the
website, "Leikah Mining" on the client's own company profile, and "ISM Supply
and Maintanance" on the Facebook page. Get this settled before creating the
listing, because changing the name afterwards triggers re-verification.

### Categories

One primary, up to nine secondary. The primary carries most of the ranking
weight, so choose the one that matches what people search for.

Suggested primary: **Equipment rental agency**

Suggested secondary:
- Construction equipment supplier
- Excavating contractor
- Diesel engine repair service
- Construction machine dealer
- Mining equipment
- Truck repair shop

Pick from Google's list; you cannot invent a category.

### Address and service area

The yard is at 7 July Street, New Industrial Area, Middelburg, 1055.

Two ways to present it:

- **Storefront**, if customers visit. Address is shown publicly.
- **Service area business**, if not. Address is hidden and only the towns
  served are shown.

For a plant hire yard, **storefront with a service area** is right: people
collect equipment and deliver machines for workshop repair, and the address
appearing publicly is an advantage.

**Confirm with the client that the address may be published.**

Service areas, from the website:

Middelburg · Witbank / eMalahleni · Hendrina · Belfast · Steelpoort · Delmas ·
Ogies · Kriel · Secunda · Lydenburg / Mashishing

### Hours

Matching the website exactly, because a mismatch is a trust signal Google reads:

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday – Friday | 07:00 – 17:00 |
| Saturday | 07:00 – 13:00 |
| Sunday | Closed |

Set **public holiday hours** as they come up. A listing showing "open" on a day
the yard is shut generates a wasted trip and a bad review.

There is also a 24-hour breakdown line. Google has no clean way to express "the
yard shuts at five but the emergency line is always answered", so put it in the
description and the services rather than in the hours.

### Contact

"NAP" — name, address, phone) is a recognised local ranking factor.

### Description

750 characters. The first 250 are what usually show. Lead with what the business
does and where, not with adjectives.

A starting point, using only facts already on the website:

> Leikah Plant Hire is a Middelburg-based plant hire, earthmoving and heavy
> mechanical contractor serving the Mpumalanga coalfields. We supply dozers,
> excavators, haulers and graders wet or dry, build and maintain haul roads, and
> run a workshop rebuilding diesel engines, transmissions, final drives and
> hydraulic assemblies. Line boring and bore welding are carried out on site or
> in the workshop. A 24-hour breakdown line is answered every day of the year.

**Have the client approve this before it goes live.**

### Photographs

The highest-effort, highest-return part of the listing. Listings with
photographs get substantially more requests for directions and calls.

There is already good material in `public/media/`:

- **Logo** — `brand/icon-512.png`
- **Cover** — a wide pit shot, `opencast-pit-overview` or `coal-bench-excavator-working`
- **Interior** — the workshop bays: `workshop-bay-plant-stripped`,
  `workshop-bays-in-use`, `workshop-component-racking`
- **At work** — `field-crew-under-machine`, `line-borer-rigged-on-boom`,
  `coal-seam-exposed`, `powertrain-lifted-by-crane`
- **Equipment** — `liebherr-excavator-low-angle`, `dozer-d10t-refurbished`,
  `excavator-with-lighting-tower`
- **Team** — `field-crew-under-machine`, `site-inspection-pit-floor`

Upload the highest resolution available, not the web renditions. The originals
are in `assest/`.

Add a few photographs every month rather than everything at once. Google reads
recency as an activity signal.

## After it is live

**Reviews are the single biggest local ranking factor** after proximity and
category. Ask satisfied clients, in person, at the point the job is signed off.
A link that goes straight to the review form makes a large difference:

```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID
```

The place ID comes from the profile once verified.

Reply to every review, including negative ones, professionally and briefly.
Google shows the replies and people read them.

**Posts.** Short updates with a photograph. They surface in the panel and signal
the business is active. A completed project, a new machine, a shutdown
availability notice.

**Keep it in step with the website.** If the hours change, or the number
changes, change both the same day. Inconsistency between the two is read as a
signal that one of them is wrong.
